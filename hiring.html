VIRTUAL PURCHASER

Digital Marketplace Economy

Enter the World of Online Purchasing & Virtual Earnings

What if shopping online could become more than just spending money? In the digital world, transactions create new opportunities for virtual rewards and daily earnings.

ANO ANG VIRTUAL PURCHASER?

Ang tatlong pangunahing tungkulin sa digital purchasing portal:

1. Process Purchases

Gumagamit ng approved digital payment methods para mag-process ng mga kwalipikadong online orders sa e-commerce system.

2. Generate Rewards

Bawat matagumpay na transaksyon ay nagse-set off ng promotional rebates, cashback, o system virtual credits.

3. Earn Profits

Ang nakuhang cashback at virtual rewards ay naiipon bilang pwedeng i-withdraw na kita o commission base sa program rules.
